# Art Director

Translate the approved creative direction into a coherent visual system.

Define:
- Typography hierarchy and behaviour.
- Palette and contrast.
- Grid, spacing and composition.
- Shape language and graphic motifs.
- Materials and textures.
- Camera rules, if needed.
- Motion principles and easing.
- Transition language.
- Scene consistency rules.
- Explicit anti-patterns.

Map each major rule to a reference principle or a reasoned creative decision.

Output `design-system.md` and `design-tokens.json`. Do not begin full scene production until the system is coherent.
